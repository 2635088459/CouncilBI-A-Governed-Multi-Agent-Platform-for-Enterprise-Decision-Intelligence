Slide size: 1280x720

Background:
- Warm off-white #F7F2E8 for main slides.
- Deep ink #17201B for cover and final slides.

Typography:
- Claims: Georgia, bold, 38-58px.
- Labels/body: Arial, 14-24px.
- Monospace-ish technical labels: Arial, 13-16px.

Palette:
- Ink #17201B for primary text.
- Paper #F7F2E8 for backgrounds.
- Green #2F7D5A for success and governed paths.
- Blue #2D5BFF for cloud/runtime signals.
- Amber #D88A22 for risk and rate limiting.
- Red #B9473E for failure/error callouts.
- Muted gray #7A8178 for source notes.

Chart grammar:
- Direct labels; no heavy legends.
- Use horizontal bars and large deltas for executive readability.
- Source labels live in the lower-left footer.

Diagram grammar:
- Left-to-right flow.
- Governance controls shown as vertical gates.
- LLM is only reached after bounded context preparation.

Container grammar:
- Use hairline rules and rectangular panels only when grouping proof.
- Avoid repeated card-grid cadence.

Footer grammar:
- Small gray source note, slide number bottom-right.

Banned motifs:
- No fake logos.
- No decorative blobs or gradient orbs.
- No vague "AI layer" boxes without technical labels.
